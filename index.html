About the Project

Problem

Flooding affects houses, roads and livelihoods in Ifakara.

Objective

To map flood risk zones using GIS techniques.

Solution

Use DEM, river data and spatial analysis.

Study Area

Ifakara is located in Morogoro Region, Tanzania. It experiences seasonal flooding due to heavy rainfall and river overflow.
