Geographic Information Systems (GIS)

GIS is a powerful technology used to capture, analyze and visualize spatial data. It helps decision makers understand real-world problems through maps and spatial analysis.

Flood Problem in Ifakara

Ifakara town in Morogoro experiences frequent flooding due to heavy rainfall, low elevation areas and overflow of nearby rivers.

Impact on Residents

Floods have caused destruction of houses, roads, agriculture fields and displacement of families. This affects daily life and economic activities.
