About Flood Risk GIS

Floods are among the most destructive natural disasters worldwide, affecting millions of people annually. Geographic Information Systems (GIS) provide powerful tools for identifying flood-prone areas, analyzing risks, and supporting emergency planning and disaster management.

Our Mission

This project demonstrates how GIS technologies are utilized to map flood hazards, assess vulnerable communities, and assist decision-makers in protecting people, critical infrastructure, and valuable property.

Key Objectives

We aim to integrate spatial data from multiple sources, apply advanced analytical techniques, and create comprehensive risk assessments that enable effective flood mitigation strategies and emergency response planning.

GIS Tools & Technologies

🗺️ ArcGIS

Industry-leading GIS software for spatial analysis, flood susceptibility mapping, and comprehensive data visualization.

🔍 QGIS

Powerful open-source GIS software for data visualization, analysis, and high-quality map creation.

📡 Remote Sensing

Satellite and aerial imagery for real-time monitoring of flood events and detecting changes in landscape.

📐 Digital Elevation Model (DEM)

Advanced terrain analysis technology that identifies low-lying flood-prone zones and water flow patterns.

📍 GPS Survey

High-precision ground data collection for field validation and verification of spatial analyses.

💧 Hydrological Analysis

In-depth study of water movement, flow patterns, and flood behavior prediction modeling.

GIS Flood Risk Workflow
