Geographic Information Systems (GIS)

Geographic Information Systems (GIS) is a computer-based tool used to collect, store, analyze, manage and visualize spatial or geographic data. It allows users to understand patterns, relationships, and trends in real-world locations through maps, satellite images and spatial analysis techniques.

Flooding Problem in Ifakara

Ifakara, located in Morogoro Region, Tanzania, is highly affected by seasonal and flash floods. These floods are mainly caused by heavy rainfall, low elevation terrain, poor drainage systems, and overflow of nearby rivers such as Kilombero River basin.

How Often Floods Occur

Flood events in Ifakara occur frequently during the rainy seasons (March–May and November–December). In some years, flooding happens multiple times within a single season, making it a serious and recurring environmental challenge for residents.

Impact on Local Communities

Flooding in Ifakara has significant impacts on human life and infrastructure. It leads to destruction of houses, damage to roads and bridges, loss of agricultural land, contamination of water sources, spread of diseases, and displacement of families. These effects slow down economic development and increase poverty levels in the area.

GIS Solution Approach

This project uses GIS techniques such as Digital Elevation Model (DEM) analysis, river buffer analysis, and land elevation classification to identify high-risk, medium-risk, and low-risk flood zones in Ifakara. The results help in decision-making for urban planning and disaster risk reduction.
